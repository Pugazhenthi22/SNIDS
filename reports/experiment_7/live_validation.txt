LOOPBACK TEST
-------------

A localhost PortScan was attempted using:

nmap -p 1-100 127.0.0.1

The scan itself completed successfully.

However, Scapy captured:

lo interface: 0 packets

Routing inspection showed that traffic destined for
172.18.167.99 is routed through the loopback interface.

Scapy's "any" interface was also unavailable in the
current WSL environment.

Therefore, the localhost PortScan test is considered
INCONCLUSIVE rather than a detection failure.

CONCLUSION
----------

The live SNIDS pipeline successfully processes real
eth0 traffic from packet capture through ML inference
and alert logging.

Loopback attack validation requires a separate isolated
test environment because the current WSL configuration
does not expose loopback traffic to the Scapy capture
used by SNIDS.
